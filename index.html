Common Questions

Everything you
might be wondering

What areas do you serve?
I serve Georgetown, Ontario and the surrounding Halton Hills area, including Acton and parts of Milton. If you're nearby but not sure, just ask!
Do I need to be home during the cleaning?
Not at all! Many of my clients give me a key or a garage code. I'll text you when I arrive and when I'm finished so you always know your home is taken care of.
Do you bring your own supplies?
Yes! I bring all my own professional-grade, eco-friendly cleaning products and equipment. If you have a specific product you'd like me to use in your home, just let me know.
What's the cancellation policy?
Life happens! I just ask for 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule. No fees, no hassle, just let me know so I can adjust my day.
Are you bonded?
Absolutely. I'm bonded so you can feel completely at ease having me in your home.
